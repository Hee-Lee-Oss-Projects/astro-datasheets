## Summary

Clear datasheets describing major open astronomy datasets (Gaia, SDSS, and more) — contents, access, license, and caveats — so learners and researchers can use them confidently.

## Scope

In scope: datasheets pointing to open astronomy datasets. Out of scope: redistributing dataset contents.

## Risk

Medium: built on openly-licensed sources with recorded provenance; educational/derivative in nature, no personal data, and no farm-/case-specific prescriptive advice. Each deliverable must cite sources and stay within established, verifiable material.

## Approach

Each task writes one datasheet following a shared template with accurate access and terms.

## Definition of shipped

Datasheets for major open astronomy datasets recording access, license, and caveats, published openly and used in teaching or research.
